equipment.json and monsters.json contain item and monster statistics taken from the
data files of the OSRS Wiki DPS calculator (https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc),
which are sourced from the Old School RuneScape Wiki (https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/).
drops.json summarises monster drop tables, and weights.json lists item weights, from the
Old School RuneScape Wiki.
